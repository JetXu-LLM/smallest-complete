## Smallest Complete

For complex, multi-step, long-running, or implementation-heavy work whose objective, authorization, and problem are clear enough to act, invoke the global `smallest-complete` skill. Pursue the smallest result that fully satisfies the user's actual request: preserve hard constraints and required behavior, maintain one coherent path, validate the observable outcome, and stop before adjacent findings become unrequested deliverables or actions. In research and analysis, explore as broadly as the requested result warrants; keep the final deliverable and any changes or external actions within scope rather than prematurely narrowing useful inquiry. Do not use the Skill to force convergence while the central objective, boundary, owner, or evidence model remains unsettled. Do not turn a requested answer, plan, review, report, prompt, or design into implementation, or a necessary fix into a broader redesign, without explicit authority.
