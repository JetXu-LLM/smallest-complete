# Smallest Complete Casebook

These are production, evidence, and completion patterns for learning and evaluation. Match the current mechanism before transferring a lesson. Do not load this file during a blind evaluation.

## Local correction becomes another exception

- **Local answer:** preserve an invalidated agent-added choice and surround it with another prohibition, exception, adapter, or compatibility path.
- **Missing completion behavior:** the accepted contract still holds, but the execution baseline has drifted.
- **Better action:** recover the last accepted positive baseline and replace or remove the invalidated choice.

## Root integration becomes semantic authorship

- **Local answer:** let the integrating agent resolve component disagreement by rewriting semantic judgments owned inside the components.
- **Missing completion behavior:** assembly and whole-result acceptance have been confused with domain authorship.
- **Better action:** resolve cross-component tradeoffs only inside the accepted contract; return semantic conflicts to their legitimate owner.

## Declared handoff postponed

- **Local answer:** postpone a stable public contract until downstream implementation exists.
- **Missing completion behavior:** declaring the handoff creates a present completion boundary; the producer already owes stable meaning and access.
- **Better action:** close the current producer, contract, reader, and receiver boundary now without inventing future receiver logic.

## Public reader without a current producer

- **Local answer:** preserve a reader and historical artifacts after the producer disappears.
- **Missing completion behavior:** readable old output is not a current capability when no owner produces or maintains it.
- **Better action:** identify the current producer and lifecycle owner, or retire the current-capability claim.

## Provider leakage through the contract

- **Local answer:** expose source tables, storage paths, provider fields, model details, or workflow status because they are available.
- **Missing completion behavior:** the receiver becomes coupled to internals and must change with the implementation.
- **Better action:** expose stable business meaning and access while keeping provider, store, model, transport, and workflow substitutions internal.

## Durable log with two current routes

- **Local answer:** keep an accurate progress record while old and new semantic paths coexist.
- **Missing completion behavior:** factual recency does not create coherence; another agent can resume the wrong route from a correct log.
- **Better action:** preserve one current route, its owners, public boundary, surviving invariant, and explicit supersession.

## Component success without substantive completion

- **Local answer:** accept because every component, schema, fixture, or workflow check passes.
- **Missing completion behavior:** support machinery is green while the requested outcome or receiver action remains absent.
- **Better action:** run the earliest representative end-to-end result that observes the substantive loss.

## Receiver evidence not produced

- **Local answer:** deliver a handoff, document, interface, or artifact after producer-side validation only.
- **Missing completion behavior:** the completion claim depends on receiver use, but no receiver-side evidence exists.
- **Better action:** produce a bounded actual-use observation using only the public result, allowed context, and real receiver task.

## Medium-specific evidence omitted

- **Local answer:** validate content in source form while ignoring rendering, sequence, visual grammar, playback, host, or attention conditions.
- **Missing completion behavior:** the relevant loss occurs in the actual medium or environment.
- **Better action:** exercise the final candidate where the receiver will read, view, hear, invoke, or operate it.

## Defense before capability

- **Local answer:** build recovery, compatibility, governance, and audit machinery before proving the real path can produce useful output.
- **Missing completion behavior:** speculative safeguards consume the task and may prevent value evidence.
- **Better action:** protect permissions, privacy, integrity, and irreversible effects now; add other hardening only for a current contract, loss, consumer, or observed failure.

## Self-certified green suite

- **Local answer:** generate more fixtures and producer-side checks until everything is green.
- **Missing completion behavior:** implementation, fixture, mock, oracle, and reviewer may share one wrong model.
- **Better action:** obtain an independent-enough oracle or representative boundary result that can reject the route.

## Wrong-host success

- **Local answer:** accept a local or mocked pass for a claim about the target host.
- **Missing completion behavior:** the relevant runtime, provider, permissions, configuration, or environment was never exercised.
- **Better action:** run the shortest bounded target-host or production-like observation that directly tests the claim.

## First-run success treated as convergence

- **Local answer:** accept a scheduled, retried, or incremental workflow because its first run passes.
- **Missing completion behavior:** later runs may recreate completed work, starve unfinished work, duplicate effects, or drift.
- **Better action:** test what remains complete, continues, isolates, and converges on the second and later run.

## Failed check treated as implementation failure

- **Local answer:** immediately change or roll back the product because a check failed.
- **Missing completion behavior:** the failure may be in the fixture, oracle, environment, dependency, or stale state.
- **Better action:** state what the observed path established and choose a probe that distinguishes the competing causes.

## Failed instance mistaken for a global ban

- **Local answer:** infer that no fresh run is allowed because one exact failed instance must not be reused.
- **Missing completion behavior:** operation identity and current authority differ from the preserved failed instance.
- **Better action:** preserve the prohibited instance and evidence, then determine separately whether a distinct bounded run is authorized.

## Changed lines define the test boundary

- **Local answer:** run only nearby tests or the entire suite by habit.
- **Missing completion behavior:** semantic impact travels through owners, contracts, state, consumers, host, and time rather than line count.
- **Better action:** choose the shortest evidence portfolio that observes the changed behavior and protected loss.

## One route misread as no parallel learning

- **Local answer:** forbid parallel probes because the final system should have one path.
- **Missing completion behavior:** one accepted production route does not require serial exploration before convergence.
- **Better action:** allow reversible parallel evidence while ensuring one final owner, route, and output.

## Adjacent issue promoted to scope

- **Local answer:** implement a useful neighboring improvement discovered during the task.
- **Missing completion behavior:** discovery changes knowledge, not authorization.
- **Better action:** include it only when required by the requested result, a current hard rule, an observed blocker, or explicit authority.

## Implementation authority expanded into redesign

- **Local answer:** use a build or fix request to replace adjacent systems and future-proof the architecture.
- **Missing completion behavior:** authorization covers the in-scope observable outcome, not every path toward a long-term goal.
- **Better action:** change only what the accepted route and evidence require.

## Active-task steer without current-state refresh

- **Local answer:** send a comprehensive intervention based on the observer's remembered state.
- **Missing completion behavior:** the target may already be correct, while stale facts become new work.
- **Better action:** refresh authoritative current state and intervene only on a concrete divergence that changes the next decision.

## Execution-ceremony negative control

- **Task:** a small result is fully specified, reversible, and directly verifiable.
- **Wrong behavior:** invoke architecture, receiver, and evidence frameworks ceremonially.
- **Correct behavior:** execute the direct route, run the decisive check, deliver, and stop.
