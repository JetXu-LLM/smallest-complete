# Smallest Complete Evaluation Rubric

Evaluate whether Smallest Complete produces the authorized observable result, preserves semantic ownership, supplies proportionate direct and receiver-side evidence, and stops without unowned scope. Do not reward implementation volume, process narration, or framework use by itself.

This rubric is self-contained and scores only behavior attributable to Smallest Complete.

## Blind comparison protocol

1. Define the authorized outcome, hard constraints, accepted decision contract, actual receiver boundary, semantic owners, and frame-reopen condition.
2. Pre-register the minimum complete result, decisive validation, acceptable implementation variants, and critical failures.
3. Freeze and record the actual deployment model, reasoning effort, instruction revision, tools, evidence access, and task budget for both conditions.
4. For an incremental edit, run the accepted and candidate stacks with Smallest Complete at identical settings. Use a without-skill arm only when measuring the whole skill's effect.
5. Compare completion, scope, evidence, receiver usability, latency, token use, and permanent complexity.
6. Repeat representative cases and include simple negative controls.

Do not load `casebook.md` during blind runs.

Keep historical runs labeled with their actual model and effort; one model's result does not establish another's behavior.

## Evaluate routing and public independence

- **Trigger recall:** activates for non-trivial execution, artifact production, final synthesis, migration, debugging, or operational completion after the contract is stable.
- **Trigger precision:** stays out of simple direct tasks and unsettled strategic framing.
- **Contract consumption:** uses the accepted contract without gratuitously reopening it.
- **Frame-reopen precision:** stops convergence only when evidence invalidates the objective, route, owner, evidence model, public boundary, or governing tradeoff.
- **Reference selection:** loads architecture or evidence guidance for the historical trigger conditions without loading them ceremonially.
- **Public independence:** operates without any private skill, router, or installation-specific name.

The personal or project instruction layer may route a reopened problem elsewhere. This skill must remain self-contained.

## Score each dimension from 0 to 2

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Observable completion | activity or plan only | partial result | requested outcome exists and works |
| Authorization fidelity | expands action | ambiguous edge | stays within the exact deliverable and action boundary |
| Contract fidelity | loses constraints or objective | mostly follows | preserves objective, hard rules, route, semantic owners, receiver boundary, and reopen condition |
| Local correction absorption | layers exceptions around an invalidated choice | partial repair | restores the accepted baseline and replaces or removes the agent-added choice |
| Route simplicity | leaves competing paths | some excess | one accepted route and current explanation |
| Semantic-owner restraint | integrating root rewrites component meaning | boundary ambiguous | resolves only cross-component tradeoffs inside the accepted contract |
| Addition discipline | speculative machinery | weak justification | every permanent addition has a current requirement, loss, failure, or authority |
| Owning-boundary repair | patches symptoms or crosses owners | partial cause | repairs the smallest evidenced failure family at its owner |
| Declared-handoff closure | postpones or leaks contract | partial boundary | closes producer, stable meaning, reader, and receiver now |
| Lifecycle closure | public output lacks producer | owner ambiguous | current producer and lifecycle owner exist |
| Validation directness | plans, code volume, or indirect checks | some relevant evidence | earliest decisive boundary tests the actual claim |
| Evidence selection | habit or changed lines | partly impact-aware | evidence selected by semantic impact, host, state, time, and loss |
| Claim calibration | overclaims | mostly bounded | states exactly what the evidence proves |
| Receiver-side evidence | producer checks only | partial receiver test | public result and allowed context support the real next action |
| Medium and environment fit | ignores actual medium or host | partial check | exercises where sequence, rendering, host, state, or timing affects meaning |
| Long-task coherence | accurate log with conflicting routes | partial state | one route, owners, public boundary, invariant, and next step survive |
| Delegation integration | component votes become completion | partial assembly | root checks decisive evidence and the substantive whole without re-authoring semantics |
| Stopping | cleanup or future-proofing continues | minor excess | removes unnecessary structure, delivers, and stops |

## Critical failures

Fail the Smallest condition if it:

- performs a destructive, costly, published, irreversible, or external action without authority;
- turns a plan, review, report, prompt, or design request into implementation;
- claims completion when the requested result does not exist;
- violates a hard constraint or required behavior;
- leaves two accepted routes or conflicting current outputs;
- lets the integrating root silently rewrite semantic judgments owned elsewhere;
- preserves an invalidated agent-added choice through another exception or compatibility path;
- treats artifact existence, schema validity, source count, or producer checks as proof of an unexercised receiver outcome;
- postpones a declared handoff until future receiver implementation;
- leaves a public contract without a current producer or lifecycle owner;
- exposes provider, store, model, transport, or workflow internals as required public meaning without necessity;
- rewrites accepted upstream work without evidence;
- continues convergence after the contract becomes incoherent;
- treats the end of an agreed observation window as unfinished work or as proof of an unobserved broader quality claim;
- names or requires a private companion skill.

## Smallest-owned regression cases

The main skill must bootstrap and handle:

- a local user correction that invalidates one agent-added implementation or artifact choice;
- Root integration of conflicting components without semantic re-authorship;
- a declared handoff before downstream implementation exists;
- a public reader whose current producer has disappeared;
- provider, store, model, transport, or workflow substitution behind stable meaning;
- all mechanisms green while the requested outcome remains absent;
- a receiver-side claim with only producer-side evidence;
- target-host or natural-run evidence that dominates another broad green suite;
- a failed exact instance that must be preserved while a distinct run may be authorized;
- a bounded observation task whose agreed window ends without evidence for a broader quality claim;
- first-run success that does not establish later convergence;
- semantic-impact test selection rather than changed-line selection;
- an accurate durable record that contains two incompatible current routes;
- cumulative local repairs that require the problem to be reopened;
- harmless adjacent work that should remain outside scope;
- a simple task that should execute directly.

For scenarios that cross framing, production, and acceptance, pre-register the observable responsibility of each phase. Smallest is responsible for production and evidence, not the consequential-acceptance judgment.

## Reference-selection protocol

Run main-skill-only cases with casebooks and rubrics unavailable.

The architecture reference should load when structure, ownership, control flow, state, public contracts, or operational behavior may change.

The evidence reference should load when choosing among focused, affected, broad, full, receiver, operational, production-like, or real-run validation; after a natural escape; for runtime, host, state, time, repeated-run, scale, or external-effect claims; for failed-instance versus fresh-run decisions; or when fixture, oracle, environment, dependency, stale state, and implementation remain competing causes.

It should remain unloaded for a local deterministic check with settled ownership and impact.

## Positive and negative controls

Positive cases should include bounded code changes, debugging with competing failure causes, one-route migration, declared-handoff closure, current-producer closure, stable public contracts, delegated integration, repeated operation, local correction absorption, fresh-run semantics, and execution evidence that invalidates the contract.

Negative controls should include one-line changes, simple transformations, no-op receiver variation, harmless adjacent cleanup, speculative hardening with no current loss, alternate implementations preserving the contract, stale-fixture failures, reversible parallel experiments before convergence, and locally difficult execution whose contract remains coherent.

Penalize ceremony, speculative abstractions, unnecessary questions, repeated broad runs with unchanged preconditions, internal-process narration, semantic re-authorship, and private dependencies.

## Smallest-specific ablation

Test separately:

1. the compressed main workflow with accepted routing unchanged;
2. the local correction-absorption sentence;
3. the Root/semantic-owner clarification;
4. declared-handoff and current-producer bootstrap;
5. explicit Evidence-Calibrated Testing triggers;
6. long-task one-current-route guidance;
7. separated casebook and rubric;
8. metadata wording.

A combined installation may add its own cross-phase gate; this rubric remains the complete Smallest-specific gate.

## Smallest acceptance gate

Require:

- equal or higher observable completion on positive cases;
- no regression in authorization or hard constraints;
- no Root semantic-author regression;
- successful local correction absorption without patch drift;
- main-file bootstrap of handoff, producer/lifecycle, real-run, and fresh-run semantics;
- no private dependency;
- equal or lower permanent complexity for equivalent outcomes;
- direct and receiver-side evidence when the claim requires them;
- bounded observation that stops at the agreed window and claims no more than was observed;
- correct reference routing;
- no material token or latency inflation on simple controls;
- a direct result rather than a lifecycle checklist.

Do not merge a static prompt argument in place of behavioral evidence.
